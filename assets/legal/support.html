Building a route

A route is a list of timed stages. Open Routes, tap New, then add a stage for each part of your session: a walking warm-up, the working stages, and a cool-down. Tap a stage to change its tempo, length or marker. Hold a stage to drag it into a different position.

Running a route

Press Start run. The timer counts down the current stage and moves to the next one automatically, with a short tone at each change. You can pause, skip a stage, or end the run early — whatever you completed is saved to your history.

Markers

Markers are a reading aid for long routes. They mark checkpoints, tempo changes and recovery points so the route is easier to follow at a glance. They do not affect the timer and carry no score.

Common questions

  • Does it need the internet? No. Everything works offline, including this page.
  • Does it track distance or GPS? No. The app measures time only, which is why it works indoors, on a track or on a treadmill.
  • Will the screen turn off during a run? No. The screen stays on while a run is in progress and returns to normal afterwards.
  • Can I turn off the sounds? Yes, in the You tab, under Preferences.
  • Where is my data? Only on this device. Removing the app deletes it permanently.

Still stuck?

Write to support@beakstormrun.com and describe what happened. Including your device model and the app version shown in the You tab helps a lot.
