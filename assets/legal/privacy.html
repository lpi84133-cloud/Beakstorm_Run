Privacy Policy

Beakstorm Run · Last updated 25 August 2026

Beakstorm Run is an offline interval workout builder. It does not require an account, and it does not send your data anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything below is kept only in the app's storage on your device:

  • Routes you build: stage names, tempo modes, durations and markers.
  • Run history: start time, duration and time spent at each tempo.
  • Preferences: appearance, sound cues, vibration and your weekly goal.
  • Your display name and profile photo, if you choose to add them.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No location or GPS access.
  • No transfer of your data to us or to third parties.
  • No internet connection is required to use the app.

Camera and photo library

Access to the camera or photo library is requested only when you choose to set a profile photo. The selected image is copied into the app's own storage and is never uploaded. You can remove it at any time from the profile screen, and you can revoke access in the system settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting a route removes it from the device. Removing the app deletes all routes, history, preferences and the profile photo permanently. Because nothing is stored outside the device, there is no copy left behind.

Children

The app collects no personal data and contains no advertising, so it is suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the app update and the date above changes with it.
